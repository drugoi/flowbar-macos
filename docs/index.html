FlowBar GitHub

YouTube audio. From your
menu bar.

FlowBar is a free YouTube audio player for macOS. Play long mixes, podcasts, and ambient tracks from the menu bar. Save them to listen offline.

Download for macOS macOS 13 or later

Listen offline

FlowBar saves audio as you listen. Set how much space your offline library can use.

Resume any track

FlowBar saves your place in each track, even when you switch to something else.

Set your pace

Paste a YouTube link to start listening. Scrub, skip 15 seconds, change playback speed, or set a sleep timer.
