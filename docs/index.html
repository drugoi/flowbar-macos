YouTube audio. From your

Keep long mixes, podcasts, and ambient tracks within reach. FlowBar saves audio for offline listening and remembers where you left off.

Download for macOS macOS 13 or later

Listen offline

FlowBar saves audio as you listen. Set how much space your offline library can use.

Resume any track

FlowBar saves your place in each track, even when you switch to something else.

Set your pace

Scrub, skip 15 seconds, change playback speed, or set a sleep timer.
